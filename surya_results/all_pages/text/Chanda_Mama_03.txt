My dear Shri Reddi,

Many thanks for your letter of 13 March sending me a set of the March issues of Chandamama in various languages. I must congratulate you for this excellent venture, particularly for your decision to bring out an edition in Sanskrit. Sanskrit has played a fundamental role in moulding our culture down through the millenia and I warmly commend your initiative in this matter.

With good wishes,

Yours sincerely,
